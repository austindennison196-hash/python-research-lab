# Hypotheses, contradictions, and next questions

## Original research hypothesis

> “Reality is a phenomenon that arises when stabilization constrains instability, allowing emergent rules to shape the formation of the universe. This is why the laws of emergence are deeply intertwined with the laws of physics.”

**HYPOTHESIS / SPECULATION.** This is the originator's motivation. None of the present algorithms or introductory physical simulations validates a claim about the universe's origins. Counting trees is not a physical measurement.

## What can be tested now

- **Mathematical question:** which invariants follow from each explicitly stated rewrite rule? Analyze balance, size, depth, branching, and reachability separately.
- **Computational question:** how do constraints on depth or branching change reachable states and empirical state entropy at a bounded generation?
- **Conjecture development:** formulate statements about an explicitly identified family and quantify over size. First search for counterexamples; retain a theorem label only after an actual proof.
- **Alternative models:** compare rules that agree on the five examples but differ later. A different generating rule with the same state set may still have different transition multiplicities and path probabilities.
- **Information question:** distinguish entropy of symbols inside one word from entropy of a probability distribution over states. Neither is an intrinsic measure of all possible “complexity.”

## Potential contradictions and ambiguities

A literal rewrite `0 -> 10` does not produce the balanced second example. Conversely, the balanced `0 -> 100` local replacement cannot explain the first step without a seed exception. The two interpretations use different meanings of zero creation. This is a formal ambiguity, not evidence that the original semantic proposal is false.

The examples alone do not select unique transition probabilities, timing, a notion of physical distance, or an energy. They also do not establish that the examples exhaust the allowed states at each generation. Those missing choices prevent unique dynamical or physical predictions.

## Requirements for a connection to physics

A physical model would require defined observables and units, an explicit mapping from structural states to measured systems, dynamics and parameters, conservation/symmetry assumptions, and predictions that can be compared with independent data. It must distinguish itself from established alternatives and specify how it can fail. Simple resemblance to a branching diagram or a quantum state label supplies none of these requirements.

## Recommended next research

1. Decide which zero-creation interpretation and seed treatment match the intended theory.
2. Define whether ordered siblings represent distinct states and why.
3. Specify stabilization as one precise constraint, then compare it with an unconstrained baseline using the same seed and counting convention.
4. Separate reachability from probability; add a transition law only when justified.
5. Prove an invariant for the constrained model or publish a reproducible counterexample.
6. Seek physical correspondence only after the mathematical model has measurable, independently motivated quantities.
